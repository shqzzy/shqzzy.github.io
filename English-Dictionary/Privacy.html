Privacy Policy for English Word Dictionary & Etymology Lookup

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how our Chrome Extension, "English Word Dictionary & Etymology Lookup" (the "Extension"), handles user data. We are committed to maintaining your privacy and ensuring a secure browsing experience.

2. Data Collection and Usage
No Personal Data Collection: The Extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, IP address, or location).
Core Functionality Data Handling: When you select or look up an English word, the extension temporarily transfers only the queried word to our database/API to retrieve its definition and etymology history. This text data is processed in real-time and is never stored, tracked, or associated with your identity on a remote server.
Local Storage: Any features related to saving words or creating custom vocabulary lists operate entirely locally on your device using the browser's native storage API (chrome.storage). This data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly choose to download/export it via the Extension's built-in export tool.
3. Data Distribution and Third Parties We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties. All operational processes are strictly aligned with the single purpose of providing vocabulary definition and etymology utilities.
4. Permissions Justification
To fulfill its purpose, the Extension requests the following permissions under strict user-initiated conditions: activeTab & scripting: Used solely to detect a highlighted word and render the lookup results panel directly on your active page when you request it.
contextMenus: Used to provide a seamless entry point via the right-click menu.
storage & downloads: Used to locally save your bookmarked words and allow you to safely download/export your vocabulary list.
5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page.
6. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer via our Chrome Web Store support channels.
