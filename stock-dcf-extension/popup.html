Metric definitions
Value Line Safety™Rank 1–5

Value Line's financial strength ranking on a 5-point scale. Lower is safer: 1 = safest, 5 = riskiest. It blends balance-sheet quality, earnings stability and debt-service capability.

  • 1 Safest — exceptionally strong finances
  • 2 High — solid finances, wide margin of safety
  • 3 Moderate — average, monitor cash flow
  • 4 Low — weaker safety, higher volatility
  • 5 Riskiest — often high leverage or unstable earnings
Expense Health Score0–10

A composite read on cost structure — expense-ratio level and trend, and whether expense growth tracks revenue growth. Scored out of 10, higher is healthier. Note this is a 10-point scale, not a percentage.

  • 8–10 Excellent — tight cost control
  • 6–7 Good — broadly healthy, room to improve
  • 4–5 Fair — moderate expense pressure
  • 0–3 Weak — heavy cost burden
P/E comparison bar

The grey tick marks the median P/E; the coloured bar is the current P/E. Above median = premium (expensive), below median = discount (cheap).
